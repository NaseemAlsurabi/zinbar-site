The 2-in-1 DJI + Walksnail Digital Ground System by ZINBAR Increases the Efficiency of Combat Mission Execution.
The system offers numerous advantages: a 180-degree rotating antenna module with a remote control, long autonomous operation time, 25 meters of cable, a 19-inch monitor, and a sealed design. The system is ready to operate in extreme conditions.

Tactical and Technical Specifications

  1. Operating time up to 24 hours
  2. Reliable communication range up to 22 km
  3. Antenna rotation angle up to 180°

Control and Video Transmission

  1. Video signal type: Digital
  2. Radio signal type: ELRS / TBS
  3. Receiver type: Digital DJI and Walksnail

Ground Station Components

  1. 19-inch monitor in a case
  2. DJI Goggles 2 / DJI Goggles 3
  3. Walksnail Avatar goggles + VRX
  4. Two TrueRC patch antennas with 4W amplifiers and two HELIX antennas with 4W amplifiers
  5. 9 or 5-meter mast in a protective cover
  6. 180-degree rotating mechanism
  7. 25-meter extension cable in shielded braid
  8. Remote controller: RadioMaster Boxer / TX-12 / TX-16 / GX-12 + TX+ Yagi antenna
  9. 50,000 mAh power batteries + 10A charger
  10. Transport case for cables, rotating mechanism, and antennas
