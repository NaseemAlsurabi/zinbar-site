Partners

ZINBAR cooperates with leading charitable foundations, volunteer organizations, and military units.

Thanks to the support of our partners, we manufacture effective ZINBAR FPV drones that help our defenders carry out combat missions with maximum precision and effectiveness.

We take pride in being trusted by top Ukrainian companies, volunteers, and elite military units who contribute to the development of military technologies. With their support, we are able to scale production, improve our designs, and promptly supply brigades with cutting-edge unmanned systems.

Drones produced by ZINBAR are actively used by units of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, the National Guard, the Defence Intelligence of Ukraine (HUR), the Security Service of Ukraine (SBU), and other special forces.

We are sincerely grateful to everyone who supports us — financially, technologically, or through information sharing — because together, we are shaping the future of Ukraine’s military.

Special thanks to Ukraine’s Defense Forces. ZINBAR stands with you and works for you.
