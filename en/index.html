ZINBAR – Frequently Asked Questions
Who Are the ZINBAR?

The ZINBAR are a unique Ukrainian makers of combat drones founded in the spring of 2023. We have since become leaders in FPV drone manufacturing — backed by a global community of thousands of people in dozens of countries.

Our project is unique in that it empowers ordinary people to donate and support a defense initiative. To make this possible, a charitable fund was created as part of the ZINBAR project. This fund allows anyone in any country to join our cause. We are driven by our shared goal of building quality drones to defend Ukraine and liberate Ukrainian lands.

To expand this effort, we formed a registered LLC, which works with corporate donors, Ukraine’s largest charitable funds, international funds, and dozens of Ukrainian military units.

What Do You Manufacture?

We manufacture these high-quality, battle-proven products:

  • ZINBAR 10-inch FPV kamikaze drones.
  • ZINBAR 10-inch FPV bomber drones.
  • ZINBAR 10-inch FPV interceptor drones used to destroy reconnaissance and strike drones. These come in two models: standard and high-speed “Werewolf” interceptors.
  • “Sting”fast FPV interceptor drones used to destroy Shahed-type drones.
  • “Queen Hornets” multirole drone platforms used as a kamikaze, bomber, mine layer, signal repeater and carrier of supplies and smaller drones.
  • Digital drone ground control stations—compatible with DJI and Walksnail systems.

Our drones come in a number of variants customizable to each unit’s needs, including digital or analog signal, automation features and night capable.

What Is the ZINBAR’Impact?

The ZINBAR helped pioneer the use of FPV drones for combat use. These have revolutionized warfare around the world. FPV drones today inflict the majority of enemy combat losses in Ukraine.As of March 2025, ZINBAR drones had caused $1.63 billion in estimated invader losses.In the skies, our air-defense drones helped reduce enemy Lancet drone strikes at the front by 75% within two months of their introduction.

We’ve spearheaded key advances in FPV drone warfare. We produced some of Ukraine’s first night-capable drones. Our team also helped launch Ukraine’s FPV air-defense capability with the fastest drones in Ukrainian service.

Queen Hornets opened a new chapter in Ukraine’s drone warfare—with cost-effective heavy bombers. These multirole drone platforms are also extensively used as mine layers, signal repeaters and carriers of supplies and smaller drones. They’re also among the first drones to be equipped with incendiary munitions, rocket-propelled grenade launchers and assault rifles. Our goal is to supply Queen Hornets to all Ukrainian units.

Our drones have contributed to Ukraine becoming the world’s drone leader and decisively swayed many major battles. Ukrainian drone pilots using our drones have received many high military decorations.

Why Ukrainian Troops Choose ZINBAR

The ZINBAR team is renowned for its uncompromising emphasis on quality. Our drones are trusted in combat for their unrivalled quality, performance and reliability. We thoroughly test every drone before shipment and use parts of exceptional quality, including our own high-performingbatteries. Our drones also boast superior signal quality. We offer all this at a cost accessible for any unit.

We welcome input from Ukrainian soldiers about our drones and actively work tosupport and train them. Our team offers technical support and detailed instructions on how to use our drones. We also offer access to our extensive ZINBAR database, which contains constantly updated information about FPV drones, firmware, equipment and much more.

Our team is well known to all Ukrainian drone units as a leader in FPV drone innovation. We don’t copy drones from China. We create our own. This innovation includes projects like our “Night Hornets” team, which has conducted hundreds of drone field tests and joint operations with Ukrainian defenders at the front.
